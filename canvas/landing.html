MONET

Impressionist on the surface, exact underneath.

CAD for people who would rather say what they want than click through a feature tree. You describe a part to your own AI agent (Claude Desktop, Claude Code, anything that speaks MCP). It writes the part as a small Python file; this page builds it, runs your checks, shows it, and lets you point at the faces you mean.

Nothing to install and no account: pressing Start makes a workspace, and its link is your key. Keep the link.

Your workspace

An LLM without a person at hand can register a workspace itself: the whole API is plain HTTPS, described for it at /api.
